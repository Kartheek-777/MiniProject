✨ ABOUT PLACEMENTCOPILOT

Building India's AI-Powered Career Readiness Network

PlacementCopilot is an AI-powered employability platform — helping students, universities, and employers connect through verified career readiness data.

Our mission

Make career readiness measurable, verifiable, and accessible to every Indian student — regardless of college tier, location, or background. One score. One platform. Real outcomes.

The problem we solve

Students don't know if they're ready. Placement cells run on spreadsheets. Recruiters can't trust resumes. PlacementCopilot fixes this with one verified readiness signal everyone can act on.

The PlacementCopilot ecosystem

Students
